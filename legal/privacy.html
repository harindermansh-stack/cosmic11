What we collect

Only what is needed to give you a reading: your name (optional), date, time and place of birth, and the questions you ask. To apply the daily free-question limit, we count requests per internet address for the current day. We do not ask you to create an account, and we never see your card or bank details (payments, when they start, are handled by Razorpay).

What happens in your browser

Your Rashi, Cosmic Score, kundli and daily horoscope are calculated on your own phone or computer. Your birth details are sent to our server only when you ask a question or request a report. Your language choice and your last report link are remembered in your browser only.

How we use it

To calculate your chart and write your answer or report. To write answers, your question and your calculated chart facts are sent to our AI provider, Groq, which processes them on its servers. We do not use your details for anything else.

Feedback

If you tap 👍 or 👎 or write a comment, we keep your rating, your comment and the question or report it refers to, so we can improve Cosmic 11. Feedback does not include your birth details. It is deleted after 180 days, or sooner if you ask.

How long we keep it

Reports are deleted automatically after 48 hours. Daily question counts are reset every day. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as internet addresses) for a short time for security.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data for advertising. We show no ads and use no tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent. Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Grievance contact: {{OWNER_NAME}}. We reply within 30 days.

Age

Cosmic 11 is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
